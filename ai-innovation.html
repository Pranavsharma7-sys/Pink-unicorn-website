AI Innovation

We help enterprises harness the full potential of large language models — from evaluation and data engineering to production-ready deployment.

LLM Evaluation

Before deploying a language model, you need rigorous, reproducible benchmarks. Our evaluation frameworks assess accuracy, robustness, bias, latency, and cost across leading open and proprietary models — so you choose the right foundation for your product with confidence.

Data Labeling & Annotation

High-quality training data is the single greatest lever in any AI pipeline. We provide expert human annotation, quality assurance workflows, and domain-specific ontology design — delivering labeled datasets that meaningfully improve model performance and reduce hallucination rates.

LLM Fine-Tuning

Generic models drift. Fine-tuned models perform. We design and execute supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) pipelines that adapt foundation models to your exact tone, domain vocabulary, and output requirements — at any scale.

RAG Optimization

Retrieval-Augmented Generation unlocks real-time, grounded answers from your proprietary knowledge base. Our team architects chunking strategies, embedding models, vector store configurations, and re-ranking pipelines that maximize retrieval precision and minimize response latency in production environments.
